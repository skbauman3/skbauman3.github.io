Privacy Policy

Surface Backup is used only by its owner to store encrypted backups of his own computer in his own Google Drive.

  • It requests only the Google Drive "drive.file" permission, which limits it to files it creates itself.
  • It does not read, collect, share, sell, or transfer any other data, and it has no users other than its owner.
  • No data is sent to anyone other than Google Drive, under the owner's own account.
